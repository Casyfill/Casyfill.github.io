Subtitle: Nine years of production ML, written up one problem at a time.
Description: Case write-ups from nine years of production machine learning at StreetEasy — entity resolution, serving under a latency budget, inferring missing attributes, and what each one taught me.

Most of what I've built belongs to my employer, so this isn't a portfolio. It's a set of write-ups about problems — abstracted enough that I can talk about them honestly, specific enough to be worth reading.

Each one follows the same shape: what the problem was, the constraint that made it hard, the decision I made, what surprised me, and what I'd do differently. The interesting part is almost never the model. It's the boundary — where a modeling choice collides with a latency budget, where a data contract prevents a failure three teams away, where deciding *not* to answer is the product decision.
